terms of service

these terms cover the ivy link checker and ivy utilities discord bots, operated by the ivy team. using either bot means you agree to these terms and discord’s applicable terms and community guidelines.

use of the bots

you must meet discord’s age requirements and have permission to use the servers, domains and ip addresses you submit. server administrators control who may manage link pools and publish panels. do not submit passwords, access tokens, private links or other confidential information.

do not use the bots for illegal activity, harassment, malware, phishing, spam, attacks, unauthorized access or infringement of other people’s rights. do not evade service limits, misuse provider accounts or use automated requests to overload the bots or their providers.

checks and generated links

filter results are category or policy estimates. a result does not guarantee access under a particular school, organization, network or account policy. unknown results are treated as blocked. links are not a security scan or an endorsement of the destination.

you are responsible for destinations you submit and the content you access. generated dns records, including their hostnames and destination ip addresses, are public. dispensing a link does not remove it from its server’s pool; other users may receive the same link.

availability and changes

the bots are provided as available, without a promise of uninterrupted service, accuracy or fitness for a particular purpose. commands, providers and features may change, and access may be limited or suspended for abuse, security or operational reasons. nothing here excludes rights or liabilities that applicable law does not allow us to exclude.

material policy changes will be dated on this page. stop using the bots if you do not accept an updated policy. server administrators can remove the bots through discord.

support

for support, complaints or data requests, contact the bot operator through the discord server where you use the bot. if you do not know the operator, ask a server administrator to put you in contact. include the relevant server id and a description of the request; never send a bot token or password.

privacy policy

information processed

  • discord provides interaction details, such as your user id, server id, permissions, selected filter and command inputs. temporary user ids help enforce cooldowns and keep private selections bound to their owner.
  • ivy utilities stores each server’s submitted link inventory and the server, channel and message ids needed to maintain its panels. pools are separate for each server.
  • during panel discovery, ivy utilities may read recent messages in channels it can access to find its own panels. other message contents are not stored in its inventory database.
  • the checker caches classification results and public freedns registry data. generation processes the ip address, prefix, filter and amount you submit.
  • aggregate server membership counts are used for the bot’s public status. the bots do not request email addresses or download full member lists for this purpose.
  • limited operational and error logs help maintain and secure the service. visiting this policy page also exposes normal connection information, including your ip address, to the hosting and network providers.

how information is used and shared

information is used to run commands, check links, generate dns records, maintain dispensers, enforce permissions and limits, and diagnose problems. submitted websites may be sent to connected filter lookup providers. generation uses sdg and cloudflare dns; public registry discovery uses freedns. discord processes bot interactions and messages under its own policies.

dispensed links and generation results are sent as private discord interaction replies. ordinary checker results may be visible in the channel where the command runs. server link pools are shared through their dispenser, so links added to a pool should not be considered confidential.

we do not sell bot user data or use it to train ai models. data may also be disclosed when legally required or necessary to respond to abuse or a security incident. this page does not load advertising or analytics scripts. other ivy website features have separate data flows and are not covered by this bot policy.

retention and protection

selection sessions, cooldowns and pagination views are short-lived runtime state. most classification results expire after about 30 minutes; unavailable results are cached for shorter periods. registry caching may last longer and refresh as it is used. expired cache entries are periodically pruned.

server link inventories and panel references remain until removed by the operator; removing the bot from a server does not currently erase that stored inventory automatically. access to credentials and databases is restricted. bot tokens are not published or included in command replies. no system can guarantee complete security.

your choices and requests

you may stop using the bots, ask a server administrator to restrict their permissions, or remove them from a server you manage. to request access, correction or deletion of data held by the ivy operator, use the support process above. we may need to verify that you control the relevant discord account or server before acting. discord messages, public dns records and records held by third-party providers may require separate removal requests.

discord’s own privacy policy and terms also apply to its platform.
